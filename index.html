Inclusive AI Research · NIHR209140

Generative AI that works with people, not just for people.

GenAI Together is exploring how generative AI can support people with learning disabilities in everyday life — through research shaped together with people with lived experience.

Project information

Research shaped together

GenAI Together — Leveraging Generative AI to Support People with Learning Disabilities in Their Daily Lives — is funded by the National Institute for Health and Care Research through its Research Programme for Social Care. The project began on 1 February 2026 and will run for 18 months, ending on 31 July 2027.

The people behind the project

The Team

Dr Misbahu Zubair

Project Lead · Senior Lecturer, Manchester Metropolitan University

Misbahu researches equitable, inclusive and usable digital systems, particularly systems that are accessible and beneficial for people with learning disabilities.

Contact Misbahu

Dr Sue Caton

Project Co-Lead · Reader in Learning Disability Research

Sue is a Reader in Learning Disability Research at Manchester Metropolitan University. Her qualitative social research addresses social and health inequalities experienced by people with learning disabilities.

Contact Sue

Dr Charlotte Hardacre

Co-Investigator · Research Associate, Manchester Metropolitan University

Charlotte is an interdisciplinary researcher focused on learning disability, family support and research culture, and a member of MMU's Learning Disabilities and Autism research cluster.

Contact Charlotte

Job Oyebisi

Co-Investigator · GenAI Together

Job's research harnesses generative AI, text-to-3D modeling, and multimodal interfaces to craft intuitive, empowering digital tools alongside people with learning disabilities and their carers.

Contact Job

Professor David Brown

Co-Investigator · Professor in Interactive Systems for Social Inclusion, Nottingham Trent University

David directs the Computing and Informatics Research Centre and researches technologies for rehabilitation, well-being, immersive systems and explainable AI.

Contact David

Dr Jordan Bird

Co-Investigator · Senior Lecturer in Computer Science, Nottingham Trent University

Jordan's interests include Artificial Intelligence, Human-Robot Interaction, machine learning, deep learning, transfer learning and data augmentation.

Contact Jordan
